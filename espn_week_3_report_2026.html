What are Expected Wins? Each week, we compare a team's actual score with every other team's actual score. Expected Wins is the sum of the percentage of opponents it would have beaten each week (ties count as half a win). For example, beating 6 of 7 other teams earns 0.86 expected wins that week. Unlike your actual record, this measures how often your score would win against a randomly selected opponent, regardless of your scheduled matchup.
